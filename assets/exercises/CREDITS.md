# Exercise demo media

Photos and instructions come from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db),
published under the Unlicense (public domain). That project restructured an earlier dataset, and the
original source of the photos isn't documented, so confirm the rights or swap in licensed media
before an App Store release.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/fetch-exercise-media.py <exercises.json> assets/exercises src/lib/exerciseMedia.ts`.
